Website Concepts · Twenty directions

Nightmare on the Crow

Twenty different looks for your new website, in two rounds. Each one is a complete, working homepage with the same information, your four nights, your $20 / $15-with-a-donation pricing, Kids Day, the food-shelf drive, and the story of three neighbors, presented ten different ways. Click any of them to view the full site, and pick the one that feels most like your haunt. Every concept also has a matching Crew page: a redesign of your volunteer log-in with a per-night station board and a one-step signup.
