Three neighbors. One haunted forest. A whole lot of coats. Built by three neighbors who love Halloween and giving back.

Nightmare on the Crow started with three neighbors who loved Halloween and wanted to give something back. Every scream on the trail helps stock a food shelf and put a coat on someone's back. Questions

Before you come out to the woodsThings parents ask us

How scary is it?

Scary. Dark tunnels, tight spaces, live actors who get close, and things that jump. It's designed to crawl under your skin. If you want the sets without the scares, come to Kids Day.Kids Day: not scary at all. Lights on, no jump scares, and the actors are in friendly mode. The evening haunt is a different animal — genuinely intense and meant for older kids and adults.

What age is it for?

There's no hard age limit, but the night trail is dark, loud and in your face. Parents know their kids best; if you're on the fence, Kids Day on Saturday the 10th is the safe bet.Kids Day is built for families with young children. The evening haunt is for older kids and adults who like being scared — you know your kid best.

What if it rains?

We're outdoors in the forest, and a little rain only makes it better. If weather makes the trail unsafe, we'll post updates on Facebook and Instagram before doors. Dress for the conditions either way.We're outdoors, so dress for the weather — boots and a jacket are a good idea in October. If conditions make the trail unsafe, we'll post updates on Facebook and Instagram beforehand.

Where do I park?

Parking is on site at 6470 Harff Road. Follow the signs and the crew directing traffic once you turn in. Wear shoes you can walk on grass and dirt in — and run in.Wear shoes for grass, dirt and a forest trail; a wagon for tired little legs isn't a bad idea.

How long does it take?

Depends how fast you walk. Between the hayride, the trail, the tunnels, the buildings and the maze, plan on a solid chunk of your evening — and leave time for the food trucks.Kids Day runs noon to 3. Plan for the hayride, the trail and the maze, plus time for food trucks and activities. Come when it works for your nap schedule.

Will the actors touch me?

Our actors don't touch guests, and we ask the same of you: no touching actors, props or sets. They'll get close. That's the point.No. On Kids Day the crew keeps a friendly distance and there are no jump scares. We just ask that kids don't climb on the sets or props.

Is the trail wheelchair or stroller accessible?

The trail is natural forest ground — roots, slopes, tunnels and corn rows — which makes it tough terrain for wheels. Reach out before you visit and we'll be honest with you about what we can do to help.

Is there food?

Yes. Just in Time Concessions and Burger Box are on site. Eat before the trail or after. Your call.Lunch on site is very much an option.
