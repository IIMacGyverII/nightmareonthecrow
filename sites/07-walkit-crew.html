          Chapter C4 — The Rules

          How we keep the woods safe.

          The Volunteer Agreement, in plain words. You agree to the whole thing when you sign on — this is the part to actually remember when a guest is screaming in your face at 9:40.

          Volunteer agreement & liability waiver

          Twelve things you're saying yes to.

          This summary is for reading. The full agreement below is what you're signing.

          1
          You're a volunteer, not an employee.Unpaid, uninsured by us, and here because you want to be. This isn't employment.
          2
          Arrive on time. Stay in your zone.Check in at the back gate, get to your station before the first wagon, and don't wander to another scene.
          3
          No physical contact with guests. Ever.No touching, grabbing, or blocking. No chasing that could make someone fall. Scare with your voice, your timing, and the dark.
          4
          No alcohol. No substances.Not before, not during. If you're impaired, you're going home — and off the crew.
          5
          Know the fire, emergency, and first-aid plan.Learn the exits on your walkthrough. If a guest is hurt or panicking, break character, call it in, and stay with them.
          6
          You accept the hazards of a haunt in the woods.Strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, and wildlife. It's real forest at night.
          7
          Keep the secrets.Trail layouts, scares, and tactics are confidential. Don't post walkthrough video or spoil scenes online.
          8
          Costumes and props aren't yours.Everything issued is company property. Return it clean-ish, report anything broken.
          9
          Photo & video release.You may be photographed or filmed in costume, and that footage may be used to promote the haunt.
          10
          Assumption of risk.You understand the risks of volunteering at a haunted attraction and accept them.
          11
          Release of liability.You release Nightmare on the Crow, its owners, and its crew from claims arising from your participation, to the extent Minnesota law allows.
          12
          Minnesota law applies.The agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
