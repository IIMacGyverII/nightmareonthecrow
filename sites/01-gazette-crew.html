Monthly build nights are back

Mid-month, mid-week, evening — one night a month through the summer for set design, props and half-baked ideas that turn into scenes. Bring a drill if you own one and an idea if you don’t. Ben has things that need to be taller and things that need to be scarier; both are volunteer positions.

Exact dates get posted here and on the crew socials the week before. Show up once and you’ll understand why the Cage looks the way it does.

— Ben & Evan

Walkthrough — pick a date

Every crew member walks the trail with the lights on before opening night: where the tunnel doors are, where the fog hides the drop-off, where you stand, where you never stand.

Pick a walkthrough date when you sign up. It’s a dropdown on the enlistment form. If none of them work, pick the closest and tell Evan.

Wear boots. It’s a forest.

What happens next

  1. You get a confirmation and your walkthrough date.
  2. Walk the trail with the lights on. Learn your station and your exits.
  3. Show up at call on your nights. Makeup, costume, a flashlight and a radio for zone leads.
  4. Be the noise in the corn until the last wagon clears.

House rules, short version

Hands to yourself. Stay in your zone. Sober. Confidential. Full text under Terms of Employment.

Terms of Employment (Unpaid)

Volunteer Agreement & Liability Waiver · Summary · The full text is below and governs

  • StatusYou are an unpaid volunteer. This is not employment, and nothing here creates it.
  • Show upArrive on time for call. Stay in your assigned area unless a zone lead moves you.
  • No contactNo physical contact with guests. No chasing, blocking or cornering that could cause a fall or harm.
  • SoberNo alcohol or substances before or during a shift. None.
  • SafetyFollow fire, emergency and first-aid procedures and the instructions of leads and the Hanover Fire Department.
  • HazardsStrobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud and wildlife. You accept them.

  • PropertyCostumes, props and masks belong to Nightmare on the Crow and are returned at the end of the night.

  • Risk & releaseYou assume the risk of volunteering and release Nightmare on the Crow and its owners from liability.
  • Governing lawThis agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.

How this works now

On the old page, the waiver and the sign-up sheet didn’t talk to each other. Here, the “I agree” box on the enlistment form is the signature, and it’s recorded with your roster entry — one step, one record.

  • Under 18? A parent or guardian signs in production. The demo form doesn’t enforce it.
  • Agreement is per season. Returning crew sign again each year.
  • Questions about the terms go to the three neighbors, not the corn.

This summary is for reading. The full agreement is what you’re agreeing to.
