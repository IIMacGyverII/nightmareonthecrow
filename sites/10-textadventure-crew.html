      cat RULES.TXT · 04 · Volunteer agreement
      
      
      The rules. Short version.
      
      
      You're an unpaid volunteer on a haunted trail run by your neighbors. This is what you're agreeing to when you check the box in ENLIST.EXE.
      
      
        
        
      • Volunteer, not employee. Unpaid, at will, no employment relationship.
        • 
        
      • Be on time. Stay put. Arrive when called and stay in your assigned area for the night.
        • 
        
      • No physical contact with guests. Ever. No chasing that could cause harm. Scare with distance, timing, and sound.
        • 
        
      • No alcohol or substances before or during your shift.
        • 
        
      • Follow fire, emergency, and first-aid procedures and the direction of the night's lead.
        • 
        
      • Confidential. Trail layouts and scare tactics stay on Harff Road. Don't post them.
        • 
        
      • Costumes and props are company property. Sign them out, bring them back.
        • 
        
      • Photo / video release. You may be photographed or filmed in costume and used in promotion.
        • 
        
      • Assumption of risk and release of liability. You accept the hazards below and release the organizers.
        • 
        
      • Minnesota law governs the agreement.
        • 
      
      
      
      Known hazards: strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, wildlife. It's a forest in October. Dress for it.
