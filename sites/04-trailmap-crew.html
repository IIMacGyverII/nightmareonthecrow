The rules of the trail

Summary of the Volunteer Agreement & Liability Waiver. Minnesota law applies.

  1. You're a volunteer. Unpaid, not employment. No wages, no benefits, all the glory.
  2. Be on time, stay put. Arrive at call time and stay in your assigned area until you're relieved.
  3. Never touch a guest. No physical contact, no chasing that could cause harm. Scare with distance, sound and timing.
  4. Sober on the trail. No alcohol or substances before or during your shift.
  5. Follow fire, emergency and first-aid procedures. Know your exit. If you hear the all-stop, the scene is over.
  6. Hazards are real. Strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud and wildlife. You accept that risk when you sign.
  7. Keep secrets. Layouts, scare tactics and what's in the last woods stay confidential.
  8. Costumes and props belong to the haunt. Borrowed, not yours. Bring them back.
  9. Photo and video release. You may end up on the socials. Consider it fame.
  10. Assumption of risk and release of liability. You're signing that you understand the above and release Nightmare on the Crow from liability under Minnesota law.
