Five scenes. One trail through the forest. It all begins with a hayride — then tunnels, winding paths, haunted buildings full of live actors, and a corn maze that doesn't want you to leave.

Tracking / troubleshooting

Before you press play

TRK 01How scary is it?

Scary. It's a haunted trail through dark woods with live actors, tunnels, haunted buildings and a corn maze — built to crawl under your skin. If you want the no-scare version, come to Kids Day on Saturday, Oct 10 (lights on, noon–3 PM).

TRK 02What age is it for?

There's no hard age minimum, but the evening haunt is built for teens and adults. You know your kid. Little ones (and nervous grown-ups) will love Kids Day — and that $5 ticket is good for $5 off an evening ticket if they decide they're brave.

TRK 03What if it rains?

It's Minnesota in October: dress for it. Light rain doesn't stop the tape. If severe weather forces a change, we'll post it on Facebook and Instagram before doors — check before you drive out.

TRK 04Where do I park?

Parking is on site at 6470 Harff Road. Follow the crew's lights when you pull in, and give yourself a few extra minutes on Saturday nights.

TRK 05How long does it take?

Depends how fast you run. Between the hayride, the trail, the tunnels, the buildings and the maze, plan on a good chunk of your evening — and arrive before the 10 PM last entry.

TRK 06Will the actors touch me?

No. Our actors won't touch you, and we ask that you don't touch them, the sets or the props. Keep your hands to yourself, keep moving, and everybody goes home happy.

TRK 07Is it wheelchair accessible?

Honestly: it's a forest trail with tunnels, hills and uneven, unpaved ground, so much of the route isn't wheelchair-friendly. Message us before you come and we'll tell you exactly what's doable and do our best to help.

TRK 08Is there food?

Yes — food trucks on site. Just in Time Concessions and Burger Box are both here, so eat before you walk in or reward yourself after.
