Production notes

Read this before you clock in

Newest first. The pinned note applies to everyone who's new this year — and to anyone who forgot where the fog machine lives.

▲ PINNED REC WALKTHROUGH TC 00:00:00:00

00 Pick a walkthrough date when you sign up

Every actor and helper walks the trail with a lead before their first night — you learn your station, your exits, where the strobes and fog are, and where not to stand. The Casting Call form has a walkthrough-date select. Pick one. Can't make any of them? Choose "None of these" and we'll sort it out at your first call time.

NOTE 03 09·26·2026 TC 00:12:41:07

03 New hours + fireworks on Oct 10

Haunt hours are now 7:30 PM – 10:30 PM, last entry 10:00 PM, all four nights. Plan to be in costume and at your station by 7:00 PM.

Saturday Oct 10: fireworks at 7:45 PM, then the haunt runs 8:00 – 10:30 PM (last entry 10). Kids Day is that same afternoon, noon–3 — if you can help with both, say so on the form.

NOTE 02 08·25·2026 TC 00:08:03:19

02 Dates we need help

Four nights, all hands. Actors, tickets, security, set design, shirt sales — every one of these needs people:

  • Fri Oct 9 — opening night
  • Sat Oct 10 — Kids Day (noon–3) + fireworks + haunt
  • Fri Oct 16
  • Sat Oct 17 — closing night

Check the Casting Board for what's open each night and grab a station. Can't do all four? Two is great. One is great.

NOTE 01 03·16·2026 TC 00:02:15:22

01 Monthly meetups are back

Set-design and idea nights: mid-month, mid-week, evenings, out at the trail. Bring ideas, gloves, and whoever you can drag along. Ben has a list of builds a mile long and the Junk & Candy Van isn't going to rust itself. Dates get posted here and in the crew group.

Safety card

The rules of the tape

The short version of the Volunteer Agreement & Liability Waiver. The full text is under the card. Checking the box on the form means you've read both.

▶ VOLUNTEER AGREEMENTSAFETY CARD · KEEP IN POCKET
  • Unpaid volunteerThis is volunteer work, not employment. No wages, no employee status.
  • On time, stay putArrive on time and stay in your assigned area for the whole shift.
  • Hands offNo physical contact with guests, ever. No chasing that could cause anyone to fall or get hurt.
  • Straight edgeNo alcohol or substances before or during your shift. Zero tolerance.
  • Fire & first aidFollow fire, emergency and first-aid procedures. Know your exits. Hanover FD is on site.
  • Known hazardsStrobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, wildlife. Watch your step; the trail doesn't.
  • Keep it secretLayouts, scare tactics and what's in the Cage stay confidential. No spoilers, no walkthrough videos.
  • WardrobeCostumes and props are company property. They stay on site when the tape ends.
  • You're on tapePhoto / video release: you may appear in footage and promo for the haunt.
  • Risk & releaseAssumption of risk and release of liability. You're choosing to work in dark woods with strobes and fog.
  • Minnesota lawThe agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
