What's waiting

It all begins with a hayride.

This isn't just a haunt. It's an experience designed to crawl under your skin — and it starts the second the wagon stops.

01

The Hayride

Your ride into the dark. The wagon rolls you away from the lights and leaves you at the trailhead with nothing but the people you came with.

02

The Haunted Trail

A winding path through the forest where the light runs out fast and something is always just off the trail — keeping pace with you.

03

Tunnels & Winding Paths

Tight, black, and longer than they look. Keep one hand on the person in front of you. Don't lose count of the turns.

04

Haunted Buildings

Rooms full of live actors and displays, each one worse than the last. Every door is a decision and none of them are good.

05

The Corn Maze

Eerie, tall, and easy to get turned around in. The rustling isn't always the wind, and the way out isn't always where you left it.

06

Live Actors

Real people, real close. Interactive scares from a volunteer crew that spends all year waiting for these four nights.

FAQ

Questions before you go

The short version: it's outdoors, it's dark, it's meant to scare you, and there are burgers.

Scary. The trail is designed to crawl under your skin: dark forest paths, tight tunnels, haunted buildings full of live actors, and a corn maze that doesn't want you to leave. If you want the sets without the scares, come to Kids Day on Saturday, Oct 10 while the lights are on.

You know your kids better than we do. The evening haunt is intense, dark, and loud, and it's built for teens and adults. For younger kids, Kids Day is the same trail with the lights on, trick-or-treating, and family activities — and the ticket is good for $5 off an evening haunt if they decide they're ready.

The whole trail is outdoors, so dress for the forecast: closed-toe shoes you don't mind getting muddy, and layers — October nights in the woods get cold fast. If severe weather forces a change, we'll post it on Facebook and Instagram before gates open.

Parking is on site at 6470 Harff Road in Greenfield. Follow the signs once you turn in, and give yourself a few extra minutes on Saturdays — the line moves, but everyone shows up at the same time.

Long enough. Between the hayride, the trail, the tunnels, the buildings, and the corn maze, plan on making a night of it. Gates open at 7:30 PM and last entry is 10:00 PM, so get in line before then — we can't let you start the trail after that.

Our actors get close — that's the point — but this is an interactive scare, not a wrestling match. Keep your hands to yourself and they'll keep theirs to themselves. If someone in your group needs to step out, tell the nearest crew member and they'll help.

Honest answer: the haunt is a natural forest trail with uneven ground, tunnels, and a corn maze, so it is not built for wheelchairs. Kids Day, with the lights on and a slower pace, is easier going. Reach out before you visit and we'll talk through what we can do.

Yes. Just in Time Concessions and Burger Box are on site. Eat before you go in or after you come out — either way, you'll have earned it.
