Before you go in

Trail Questions

How scary is it, really?

It's a real haunt: dark woods, tight tunnels, live actors, jump scares, and a corn maze that doesn't care about your sense of direction. It's designed to crawl under your skin. If you want the sets without the scares, the Daylight Trail on Saturday, October 10 is the lights-on version.

What age is it for?

Parents know their kids best. As a rule of thumb, the evening trail suits teens and adults; younger kids (and nervous grown-ups) love the Daylight Trail at noon on October 10. Kids Day tickets are also worth $5 off an evening ticket if they decide they're brave.

What if it rains? Or snows? It's Minnesota.

The trail is outdoors and runs in October weather. Dress warm, wear shoes you don't mind muddying, and check our Facebook and Instagram the day of for any weather updates.

Where do I park?

On site, in the parking field at 6470 Harff Road. Follow the signs and the crew with flashlights. Then walk to the Ticket Barn, where the hayride begins.

How long does the trail take?

Long enough. The path winds through the woods, the tunnels, the haunted buildings and the corn maze, and groups move at the pace of their slowest screamer. Plan on a good chunk of your evening, and get in line before the 10 PM last entry.

Will the actors touch me? Can I touch them?

Our actors won't touch you, and we ask the same of you: no touching actors, props or sets. They're neighbors and volunteers, and the skeleton in the cage took Ben a long time to build.

Is the trail wheelchair accessible?

It's a natural forest trail with uneven ground, roots, tunnels, and very little light, so it isn't paved or fully accessible. The Daylight Trail is easier to navigate, and we'll do what we can — reach out on our socials before your visit and we'll talk it through.

Is there food?

Yes. Just in Time Concessions and Burger Box park by the Ticket Barn on trail nights. Eat before you go in; nobody wants a burger in the corn maze.
