    Conditions of Service

    The Volunteer Agreement, set down plain. The full text is folded beneath.

    1. You are a volunteer, not an employee. The work is unpaid and this is not employment. You give your nights freely, and the neighbors are grateful for every one.
    2. Arrive on time; keep to your assigned area. Be at your station before the first wagon rolls, and stay in it till the last group is through. The trail only works if every post is held.
    3. No physical contact with guests. None. No touching, no grabbing, no chasing that could cause harm. Scare with the dark, the voice, the timing. Guests are asked for the same courtesy.
    4. No alcohol or substances. Not before, not during. A hand under the influence is sent home and struck from the ledger.
    5. Follow fire, emergency and first-aid procedures. Know where the exits are, where the extinguishers hang, and who is In Charge that night. If a guest is truly hurt, the scare stops and the lights come up.
    6. Know the hazards of the field. Strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, and the wildlife of the Crow River bottoms. Wear boots. Watch your footing.
    7. Keep the layouts and scare tactics confidential. What happens on the trail stays on the trail until the last lantern is out. Don't post the map; don't spoil the Cage.
    8. Costumes and props belong to the haunt. They are company property; return them clean and whole at the end of your night.
    9. Photo and video release. You may be photographed or filmed in costume, and the haunt may use those images to promote the trail.
    10. Assumption of risk and release of liability. You accept the risks of working a haunted trail at night and release the haunt and its neighbors from liability for them, as the full agreement sets out.
    11. Minnesota law governs. The agreement is read under the laws of the State of Minnesota.
