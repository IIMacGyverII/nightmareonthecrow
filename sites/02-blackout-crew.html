Volunteer agreement & liability waiver

The rules of the dark.

The short version. The full agreement is below it, and you agree to the full one when you sign up.

  1. You’re a volunteer.

    Unpaid. Not employment. You’re here because you want to be, and you can leave when you need to.

  2. On time. In your zone.

    Arrive when you’re scheduled and stay in your assigned area. If you move, we don’t know where you are — and neither does first aid.

  3. No touching. No chasing that hurts.

    No physical contact with guests, ever. No chasing that could put anyone on the ground. Scare them; don’t hurt them.

  4. Sober.

    No alcohol or substances before or during your shift.

  5. Know the procedures.

    Fire, emergency and first aid. You’ll get them at your walkthrough. Ask if anything’s unclear.

  6. The hazards are real.

    Strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, wildlife. You accept these risks when you volunteer.

  7. What’s in the woods stays in the woods.

    Layouts, scare tactics and what’s in each building are confidential. No spoilers, no behind-the-scenes posts without asking.

  8. Costumes and props aren’t yours.

    They belong to the haunt. Bring them back the way you found them, or tell us what broke.

  9. You may be photographed or filmed.

    And we may use it — socials, the website, next year’s promo.

  10. Assumption of risk. Release of liability.

    You take on the risk of volunteering and release the haunt and its organizers from liability. Governed by Minnesota law.
