What this is

In their words

“This isn’t just a haunt. It’s an experience designed to crawl under your skin.”

Three neighbors. One forest by the Crow River. A hayride in, and then a trail of tunnels, buildings and corn full of live actors who know exactly where you are.

Questions in the dark

FAQ

How scary is it?

Scary. Live actors, tight tunnels, dark woods, jump scares — it’s designed to crawl under your skin. If you want the gentle version, Kids Day on Oct 10 is a lights-on walk with trick-or-treating.

What age is it for?

The evening trail is built for teens and adults. Parents know their kids best — if they’re young or easily spooked, bring them to Kids Day instead. It’s $5, the lights are on, and nobody chases anyone.

What if it rains?

It’s an outdoor trail through real woods, so dress for Minnesota in October: layers, closed-toe shoes you don’t mind muddying. For any weather-related updates on show nights, check our Facebook and Instagram before you head out.

Where do I park?

Head to 6470 Harff Road, Greenfield, and follow the signs — our crew will point you where to go. Come a few minutes early on Saturdays; they fill up.

How long does it take?

Plan on a good chunk of your evening from the hayride to the far side of the corn — how long depends on how fast you walk, and how fast you run. Gates are open 7:30 to 10:30 PM (8:00 on Oct 10, after the fireworks); last entry is 10.

Will the actors touch me?

Our actors don’t touch guests. Please return the favor — hands off the actors, the sets and the props. They took a long time to build, and some of them bite back.

Is it wheelchair accessible?

The trail crosses uneven forest ground, tunnels and corn, which makes the evening haunt tough for wheelchairs and strollers. Kids Day is easier going. Message us before you visit and we’ll do everything we can to make it work.

Is there food?

Yes. Just in Time Concessions and Burger Box are on site. Eat before the trail or after — your stomach, your call.
