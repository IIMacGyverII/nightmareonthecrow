    How it works

    1. 1Pick your nights. One is fine. All four gets you a very nice hoodie. (Kidding. Maybe.)
    2. 2Choose a zone and station. Orange badges on the board are open right now.
    3. 3Pick a walkthrough. Lights on, 45 minutes, meet your zone lead.
    4. 4Agree and submit. Your waiver and your signup are the same step.

    Night of

    • Be at your station, in costume, by 7:00 PM. Guests start at 7:30.
    • Crew parking is on site at 6470 Harff Road. Follow the crew signs.
    • No touching guests, ever. Scare them; don't grab them.
    • Dress warm under the costume. October in the woods gets cold fast.

    Volunteer agreement

    The short version

    What you're agreeing to when you check the box. The full agreement is in the expander below — read it once.

    Minnesota law applies
    • Unpaid volunteerThis is volunteering, not employment. No wages, no benefits.
    • Show up, stay putArrive on time and stay in your assigned area until your lead releases you.
    • No physical contactNever touch a guest. No chasing that could cause a fall or harm.
    • No alcohol or substancesNot before, not during. Zero tolerance on haunt nights.
    • Fire, emergency & first aidFollow procedures and your zone lead's calls. Know your exits.
    • Known hazardsStrobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, wildlife.
    • Keep it secretLayouts, scares, and tactics stay confidential. Don't post walkthrough video.
    • Costumes & propsCompany property. Sign them out, bring them back.
    • Photo & video releaseYou may appear in our photos and video, and we can use them to promote the haunt.
    • Assumption of risk & releaseYou accept the risks of a night-time outdoor haunt and release the haunt from liability.
    • Minnesota lawThe agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
    • One checkboxChecking "I agree" on the signup form is your signature for the season.
