    Volunteer Agreement & Liability Waiver

    The short version.The short version.

    You agree to all of this when you tick the box above. The full agreement is below it.You agree to all of this when you tick the box above. The full agreement is right below.

    • Unpaid volunteer. This is not employment. No wages, no benefits, lots of gratitude.
    • Show up, stay put. Arrive on time for your call and stay in your assigned area until released.
    • No physical contact with guests. Ever. No chasing that could cause a fall or harm. Get close; never touch.
    • No alcohol or substances before or during your shift. You'll be sent home.
    • Fire, emergency and first aid. Learn the procedures at your walkthrough and follow them. Radios and the In Charge station run the night.
    • Know the hazards: strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud and wildlife. It's a forest in October.
    • Keep it secret. Layouts, scare tactics and what's behind the Black Sheets stay confidential. No spoilers online.
    • Costumes and props belong to Nightmare on the Crow. Return them the way you found them (or better).
    • Photo and video release. We may use images from the trail that include you, in costume or not.
    • Assumption of risk and release of liability. You accept the risks of volunteering at a haunted attraction and release the organizers from liability, to the extent Minnesota law allows.
    • Minnesota law governs the agreement.
