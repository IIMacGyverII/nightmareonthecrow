How this works

One form replaces the old spreadsheet, the waiver form, and the sign-up sheet. Nobody edits anybody else's row.

  1. 01Pick your nights. One is fine. Four is legend.
  2. 02Choose a zone and a station. Tapping an open slot on the call sheet fills these in for you.
  3. 03Pick a walkthrough evening. You'll walk your station before the public ever sees it.
  4. 04Agree to the Volunteer Agreement. Then you're on the roster, and the call sheet updates.

Questions before you commit? Ask on the crew Facebook group or grab a neighbor at the next build night.

The agreement

Volunteer Agreement & Liability Waiver · Summary

The short version of what you're agreeing to when you tick the box. The full text is below and applies in full; this summary is for reading on a phone in a cold field.

  1. 01Unpaid volunteer, not employment. No wages, no benefits, no employment relationship.
  2. 02Be on time, stay in your area. Arrive when called, work your assigned station, don't wander the trail.
  3. 03No physical contact with guests. No touching, no grabbing, no chasing that could cause anyone to fall or get hurt.
  4. 04No alcohol or substances before or during your shift. None.
  5. 05Follow fire, emergency and first-aid procedures. Know your exits. When a lead says stop, the scene stops.
  6. 06Known hazards: strobes, fog, darkness, confined spaces, uneven ground, weather, mud, wildlife. It's a forest at night. You're choosing to be in it.
  7. 07Keep it secret. Trail layouts, scare tactics and scene builds are confidential. Don't post the map.
  8. 08Costumes and props are company property. Borrowed, returned, not souvenirs.
  9. 09Photo and video release. You may be photographed or filmed in character and used in promotion.
  10. 10Assumption of risk and release of liability. You accept the risks of volunteering at a haunt and release the organizers accordingly.
  11. 11Minnesota law governs the agreement.
